About Skill to Market

Skill to Market is a digital platform designed to help skilled youth showcase their talent, products and services to potential customers.


The platform focuses on skills such as handicrafts, tailoring, art, designing, handmade products, photography and frame making.


Our goal is to create better market linkages and livelihood opportunities for skilled youth by making their products visible to a wider customer base.


Technology is used as an enabler to connect skilled youth with markets and customers.
