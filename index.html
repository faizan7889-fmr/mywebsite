About & Aim

Why Skill to Market exists

Many young people in Jammu have real skills but no way to reach buyers. At the same time, schools, shops and programs struggle to find reliable local makers.

Our aim: to make every skilled youth in Jammu visible, trusted and able to earn from their skill.

We build a simple, free and local marketplace where skill meets demand — so talent no longer goes unused.

💰

Income for youth

Help skilled youth find regular orders and build a sustainable income.

🛡️

Trust in the market

Verified local profiles so buyers order with confidence.

🌾

Inclusion

Special focus on rural areas and women in tailoring, crafts and more.

How it works

Simple for sellers. Simple for buyers.

Register once, get your own page, and start connecting.

🧵 For Sellers (skilled youth)

  • 1Register free with your skill and area.
  • 2Get your own profile page — add about, experience, prices and products.
  • 3Receive enquiries from buyers directly on your dashboard.

🛍️ For Buyers

  • 1Register free as an individual, school, shop or program.
  • 2Browse sellers by skill and read their profiles.
  • 3Send an enquiry for bags, uniforms, gifts and more.
