Your skill deserves a real market.

Skill to Market connects skilled youth of Jammu — tailors, artisans, makers — directly with buyers, schools, shops and programs. Free to join. No middlemen. No hidden bills.

The gap we are solving

Jammu has talent. What it lacks is a trusted bridge to buyers.

🪡

Skills without income

Many youth, especially women in rural areas, are skilled in tailoring and crafts but don't know how to turn it into earning.

🔍

No access to buyers

Local orders from schools, shops and programs exist, but there's no easy way to connect.

⚠️

Platforms don't fit

Existing platforms need GST bills, charge to open accounts and lack trust for first-time sellers.

How it works

Four simple steps from skill to order.

1

Create free profile

Add your skill, a few photos and your area. No fee.

2

Get verified

Local partners (Youth Clubs, ITIs, Panchayats) vouch for you — building trust.

3

Receive orders

Buyers browse and place orders for bags, uniforms, gifts and more.

4

Earn & grow

Deliver, get paid, and build your reputation with reviews.
